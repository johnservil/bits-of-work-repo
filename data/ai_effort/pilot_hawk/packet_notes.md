# Packet notes: state of the art as of 2026-06-30 (pre-cutoff packet only)

1. HAWK (2022/1155; spec v1.1, Feb 2025) bases signatures on rank-2 module-LIP over power-of-two cyclotomic rings; the public key is a Gram matrix (Hermitian form) of a lattice isometric to O_K^2, the secret is the basis change Q. Key recovery is the search module-LIP problem (smLIP).
2. Design-level assumption: omSVP (one-more SVP) for forgery; smLIP for direct key recovery. Claimed security rests on lattice-reduction experiments on the key-recovery lattice (BKZ-style, dimension ~2n for HAWK-n).
3. Generic Z^n results: Z-LIP is solved by BKZ with blocksize about n/2 (heuristic, DPPvW22; provable in 2023/447 with SVP calls in dimension <= n/2+1); 2024/601 extends provable halved-dimension reduction to NTRU and near-hypercubic lattices (heuristically ~4/9 n for primal attacks). Szydlo-style search-to-distinguish reductions: 2025/1204 gives full HAWK key recovery from fewer than 2d^2 distinguishing calls (d = ring degree), so distinguish-LIP is the crux.
4. Automorphisms: 2023/1735 shows Z-LAP (find a nontrivial automorphism) is as hard as Z-LIP via randomization by random automorphisms. 2024/1173 finds a (pseudo) symplectic automorphism of the HAWK-type module lattice that invalidates the original omSVP definition (no direct attack; the HAWK team amended omSVP in the round-2 spec).
5. 2025/928: if a nontrivial automorphism of the underlying integer lattice is known, smLIP over a power-of-two cyclotomic ring reduces to LIP of at most half the rank, speeding key recovery at least quadratically (halving security bits). The paper states this does not affect HAWK as specified; it flags the automorphism group as an open attack avenue.
6. Rank-2 module-LIP over totally real fields is broken in (heuristic) polynomial time (2024/441, via relative norm equations / sums of two squares); 2024/1173 gives a provable deterministic polynomial-time version; 2025/280 extends to all number fields with a real embedding (quantum poly-time heuristic and provable; classical heuristic under 2-transitive Galois group).
7. These do not apply to CM fields such as the HAWK ring: there the analogous equation is a sum of four squares x^2+y^2+z^2+t^2=q, whose solution set is argued too large to enumerate (2025/1095).
8. Reductions for CM fields: 2024/1147 (= 2025/287) reduces rank-2 module-LIP over CM fields, and HAWK in particular (uniformly), to a principal ideal problem in a quaternion algebra (nrdPIP, Eurocrypt 2025). 2024/1148 gives an elementary reduction to finding a square basis in Hermitian decomposition lattices.
9. 2025/1448 gives dimension-reducing algorithms (to 1/2 or 1/4 dimension) for approximate HSVP in quaternion ideal lattices, with a quantum poly-time algorithm given a number-field HSVP oracle; it is not an attack on HAWK by itself.
10. 2026/1318 (posted 2026-06-25, updated 2026-06-30) sketches a classical key-recovery algorithm via re-randomizing the nrdPIP instance with unimodular U and a Lenstra-Silverberg subfield approach, under four heuristics; the authors' own update states the main algorithm appears to run in super-polynomial time, and nothing was verified experimentally. It is not an established break.
11. Distinguishing / arithmetic invariants: genus of the HAWK lattice has huge mass (2025/215), so genus alone does not distinguish; spinor/special-genus analogues are efficiently computable for HAWK's lattice but give no practical advantage (2025/940; 2024/1475 for spinor genus of binary forms).
12. Hull attacks (2023/194; module version 2025/1376) give a halved exponent constant for some Construction-A lattices but Z^n, unimodular and modular/Barnes-Wall families resist them; 2026/890 evaluates its definite/indefinite-form methods on HAWK and finds no compromise (they do break DEFI).
13. Side-channel and implementation attacks: power analysis of the HAWK sampler (2024/1248); algebraic key recovery from sampler leakage (2026/699, full recovery from a single signature with full coefficient leakage; noisy-sign variants need hundreds to ~13k signatures).
14. Protocol-level: HAWK is not BUFF-secure as specified because of unchecked degenerate public keys (2026/1298); quantum security reduction to omSVP in the QROM (2023/711).
15. Group-action/other LIP results: LIP group action fails weak unpredictability/pseudorandomness with 2-3 same-secret instances (2025/516); double-LIP attack (2026/722); search-LIP is not NP-hard unless PH collapses (2026/1139).
16. Best known concrete attack cost on HAWK key recovery as of 2026-06-30 (from the packet): lattice reduction (BKZ with SVP oracle) on the ~2n-dimensional key lattice at blocksize about n/2-scale; the spec's security estimates are unchanged; no published polynomial-time or sub-exponential break of HAWK-512/1024 key recovery is established in this packet.
17. Known reduction directions not yet realized as concrete cost improvements for HAWK: (a) automorphism-assisted halving of rank (2025/928, conditional on knowing an automorphism); (b) nrdPIP/quaternion route (2024/1147, 2025/1448); (c) distinguish-to-search (2025/1204).
18. Open questions at cutoff: whether HAWK's key lattice has (or can be made to exhibit) exploitable nontrivial automorphisms beyond the symplectic one; whether the nrdPIP route or subfield tricks yield sub-exponential algorithms for the actual HAWK fields; exact SVP-dimension needed after any symmetry-based reduction.
