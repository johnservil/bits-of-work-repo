# Comparator records: notes (agent pass, 2026-10-08/09)

34 records in `records/comparator/*.yaml`. All have `verified: false`. They were
generated by `_scratch/gen.py` (stdlib only; rerun with
`python3 -I records/comparator/_scratch/gen.py` from the repo root).
`_scratch/pdftext.py` is a small zlib-based text extractor I used on PDFs that
WebFetch saved locally. It worked for the RSA-768 paper (CWI copy) and the
240-digit paper (NSF PAR copy). ePrint PDFs returned 403, so for those I only
read the abstract pages.

## Comparability caveats for analysis/

- The units differ by era: MIPS-years (1994-1999), single-core CPU-years (taken
  as core-years for 1999), 2.2 GHz Opteron-years (2005, 2009), Xeon Gold 6130
  core-years (2019-2020), Ivy Bridge core-hours (GF(2^30750)), and GPU-years
  on B200/GB200/GB300 or H100 (2026). Normalization belongs in analysis/.
- For RSA-260, the author's estimated CPU equivalent of about 7,010 core-years
  is mentioned in the notes field only. I left `reported_core_years` null
  because the figure was estimated, not measured.
- The RSA-240 total is 1000 core-years in the paper but 900 (sieving plus
  matrix) in the announcement. DLP-240 is 3200 in the paper and 3100 in the
  announcement. The record files use the paper figures.
- DLP-768 is reported as 5300 core-years (cited in the DLP-240 paper) or 6600
  core-years (Wikipedia). The file uses 5300.
- Some `reported_core_years` values are derived:
  - DLP-596: sum of the stated phases.
  - GF(3^(6*97)): days × cores / 365.25.
  - GF(2^6168) and HFE Challenge 1: hours / 8766.
  - The SIKE values: minutes or hours converted to years.
- The isogeny and multivariate entries are structural breaks. Their compute
  ranges from minutes to a weekend on one machine, while factoring and FF-DLP
  records take thousands of core-years or tens of GPU-years. That contrast is
  the reason these records are included.

## Gaps / not done

- Several compute figures come only from Wikipedia: RSA-129, 130, 140, 160 and
  200, 2^1061-1, GF(2^6168), GF(3^(6*509)) and GF(2^9234). For these the
  primary announcements, mostly NMBRTHRY posts, were not read. Confidence is
  0.55-0.7.
- RSA-160, RSA-576, DLP-530, 2^1039-1 and both medium-prime records have null
  compute.
- Rainbow `instance_size` is null because I did not read the parameter values.
- The 2^1039-1 date is from Zimmermann's page. The SIKEp751 Magma timing is
  from a GitHub README table, not the paper.
