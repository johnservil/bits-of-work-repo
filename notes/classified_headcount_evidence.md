# Evidence on classified / government cryptanalytic and mathematical headcount

Compiled 2026-10-09 for the public-key assumption effort study. Purpose: collect evidence, not
produce the final estimate. Every number has a source, URL, publication date and a
reliability tag:

- **official**: published by the agency or government itself
- **leaked-official**: a government document that leaked and was published by a reputable outlet
- **journalist estimate**: a reporter's figure, often from unnamed former officials
- **historian**: a published history, including declassified agency histories
- **secondary**: a summary of someone else's figure, where I could not open the primary source
- **unverified**: anecdote, blog comment, or a figure that could not be traced

"Fetched" means I opened and read the source in this session. "Via search" means the figure
came from a search-engine summary or a secondary quotation, and the primary source was not
opened. Arithmetic marked **[inference]** is mine, not the source's.

**Bottom line for mathematicians:** NSA has never published a headcount. The only
NSA-sourced flow figure is about 50 hires a year (Schatz, about 2003). If the career is
roughly 25-30 years long, that implies a stock of about 1,250-1,500 **[inference]**, but
attrition and hiring are not steady. The 30-40 a year estimate from 2013 would give a smaller
stock. The 600 figure is from 1999 and anonymous.

## Gaps and what cannot be known

1. **No agency has published a mathematician or cryptanalyst headcount.** NSA's "largest
   employer" claim has never had a number attached. The ~1,000 figure has no traceable
   primary source. The ~600 figure is an anonymous 1999 account.
2. **There is no split by assumption class.** Nothing public says how classified effort
   divides among ECDLP, lattices, codes, factoring/DLP or symmetric work. The FY2013 CES
   project text mentions "public key" generically, and its specifics are redacted. Soliloquy is
   the only public window onto classified *lattice* cryptanalysis (GCHQ, 2010-13).
3. **Mathematical cryptanalysis is not separated from exploitation.** S31/CES (">1,500";
   ~$1B) mixes mathematical cryptanalysis with reverse engineering, implementation attacks,
   key theft, HPC procurement and CNE support. Only part of it is comparable to academic
   assumption-level cryptanalysis.
4. **Dates are mismatched.** The best leaked numbers are from FY2011-13. Mathematician hiring
   figures are from ~2003 and 2013. Workforce figures are from 2022-2026. The NSA math grant
   program shrank after 2016, and NSA cut ~2,000 staff in 2025.
5. **IDA C&C staffing** is known only as ">70 PhDs" at the two CCRs (undated) plus a contract
   value. CCS Bowie has no headcount.
6. **Non-Five-Eyes** (China, Russia, France, Israel): no cryptanalyst counts exist. The only
   numbers are broad SIGINT workforces (DGSE technical directorate 2,000; FSB 16th Center
   >=566; Unit 8200 ~5,000+; 3PLA "up to 100,000"). Their cryptanalytic share is unknown.
7. **The quality and focus of classified work cannot be observed.** Historical leads (DES
   ~16-18 years, GCHQ public key ~3-4 years, Soliloquy ~2-5 years) are anecdotes chosen
   because they were later declassified, which is a selection bias.
8. Not retrieved in this session: the ISC 2023-25 GCHQ staffing annex (403), the ASD 2024-25
   annual report workforce table (timeout), the Schatz NAP transcript text (JS-rendered),
   Hinsley's Bletchley personnel tables, and Johnson Books II-IV (nsa.gov blocked; the GWU
   copies are scanned with no text layer).

## The 10 most decision-relevant numbers

1. **$119.3M (FY2013 request); $146.4M (FY2011); $128.5M (FY2012)**: NSA R&T line
   "Cryptologic Math & IDA Research". Leaked-official (FY2013 CBJ Exhibit 13). This is the
   closest budget proxy for classified mathematical cryptanalysis research.
2. **$1,003.2M (FY2013)**: the Cryptanalysis & Exploitation Services expenditure center.
   Leaked-official. Mixes math, HPC hardware and exploitation.
3. **240 civilian FTE plus 2 military on $39.4M (FY2011)**: CES "Analysis of Target Systems"
   project, which includes public-key exploitation. Leaked-official. It is the only leaked
   project with both an FTE count and a dollar figure.
4. **>1,500 people in S31 (NSA Office of Cryptanalysis and Exploitation Services), 2013**:
   journalist estimate (Aid, from former officials).
5. **34,901 CCP personnel; 21,575 NSA (NIP-funded), FY2013**: leaked-official. Use these
   instead of the "35,000 code-breakers" press framing.
6. **~50 mathematician hires per year, >60% with PhDs (NSA, ~2003)**: official, from the
   Mathematics Research Group chief. Compare 30-40 per year (AMS, 2013, secondary).
7. **Research Directorate: 5 departments x 100-200 people (2022)**: journalist, from NSA
   interviews. The math department is one of the five, so roughly 100-200 research-directorate
   mathematicians **[inference]**.
8. **IDA C&C: >70 PhD mathematicians and computer scientists at CCR-P plus CCR-L
   (undated); ~$713M contract over 2016-2025 (~$71M/yr)**: official (IDA; GAO-22-105278).
9. **Heilbronn: >30 Research Fellows and >45 members, each half-time on GCHQ-directed
   research (current)**: official. That is about 20-25 classified FTE from academic
   mathematicians **[inference]**.
10. **NSA total workforce ~39,000 (2024), minus ~2,000 in 2025; GCHQ 7,082 FTE (2022); CSE
    4,178 (2025-26); DGSE technical directorate ~2,000 (2014)**: official, journalist and
    secondary mix. These give whole-agency scale only. No cryptanalyst share is published.
