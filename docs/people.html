Who did the looking?

Counted cryptanalysis by author, field and period: the few who wrote the most, and the many who wrote one paper

This is a very early work in progress, and errors are still being addressed. Each counted paper gives its authors equal shares of its class weight, after counting each work once (a preprint of a counted paper adds nothing; a journal extension adds one third). These paper credits add up to the paper counts on the summary page. The papers view instead gives every author the paper's whole class weight. Both measure published output only. They do not measure time spent, the importance of a paper, or work that was never published.

Identities have not been checked by a person. Authors were matched to DBLP person records where possible. Name strings that differ from exactly one DBLP person only in dashes, name order or a homonym number are merged into that person here. The remaining unmatched strings (marked †) stay separate, and a failed match can still split one person into two or merge two people into one. To limit the effect of these errors on individuals, two kinds of identity are never named or ranked here: people with fewer than 3 counted papers, and 12 identities on ambiguous names. The second group is common Chinese names where DBLP has not yet assigned papers to a specific person (its unnumbered “disambiguation” profiles and very large catch-all profiles), or where we matched only the spelling. Both groups still count toward totals, contributor counts and the “everyone else” row. Each profile says how many of its papers were matched to the person by name alone. 2026 is a partial year.
